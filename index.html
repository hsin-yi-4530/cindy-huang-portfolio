Reach businesses researching cloud migration and related solutions across Taiwan and Malaysia.

Optimization approach

Creative TestingRetargetingLead Quality Analysis
Explore results & learning

Objective & approach

Promote managed cloud services through LinkedIn awareness and lead generation campaigns, supported by Google Search. Messaging paired the AWS MSP award with cost optimization, security and service-package offers.

How the channels connected

Google Search and LinkedIn ads supported discovery and lead generation. Week 1 social and EDM content introduced AWS award recognition and cloud optimization benefits. Website assets explained cloud challenges and the service offering.

Creative comparison
