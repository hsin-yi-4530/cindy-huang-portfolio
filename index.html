Explore campaign approach

Objective

Reach businesses researching migration, pre-migration and GenAI in Malaysia, and brand and GenAI solutions in Taiwan.

Optimization approach

  • Reviewed search terms and keyword performance to pause costly, low-engagement terms and exclude irrelevant searches.
